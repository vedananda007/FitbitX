About FitbitX

FitbitX is a modern fitness and gym management web application designed to help users manage their health and fitness journey effectively. Our project provides features like workout tracking, membership management, trainer details, fitness plans, and progress monitoring. The main goal of FitbitX is to create a user-friendly platform that improves gym management and helps users stay motivated toward achieving their fitness goals.

This project was developed as part of our academic case study by a team of passionate Computer Science students. We focused on creating a clean design, smooth user experience, and practical features that solve real-world fitness management problems.
